# Quiett meditation catalog

A brief for Ross to hand a voice artist. The artwork for every entry already exists in `assets/track-covers/`; `meditations-sheet.jpg` shows them all together and `meditations.json` holds the same data in machine-readable form.

## Principle

- **Image first, title from the image.** Each meditation starts from its illustration, and the title and theme come from what the picture shows. That keeps cover, name and script as one idea.
- **Pure sounds get literal images.** Ambient sounds and healing tones (rain, waves, birds, singing bowl, piano, tanpura, cloud pad) use a plain image of the sound source rather than a landscape metaphor. Those are *not* voice tracks and are not part of this brief.

## Script premises for the 14 new meditations

Style rules for every script:

- About **1:45 of voice** inside a 2-minute track. Leave the pauses in the recording; don't edit them out.
- Calm, plain, second person ("you"). The listener has just got up and is standing or moving slowly, so no instructions to lie down or be seated.
- No medical or therapeutic claims: nothing about treating, curing or healing anything. Describe experiences, not outcomes.
- End softly so the track can hand back to the app without a hard stop.

### Above the Mist

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-02.jpg`. Theme: Watching thoughts settle like fog in the valleys.

**Premise.** From a high ridge the listener looks down on valleys filled with fog. Each thought is invited to drift down into the mist and settle there, while attention rests on the clear air and the slow breath up top. The piece ends with the fog thinning as the sun comes over the ridge.

**Voice.** Unhurried and spacious. Leave long pauses after each image so the listener can see the valley.

### Among the Pines

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-04.jpg`. Theme: Grounding: tall, rooted, breathing with the trees.

**Premise.** The listener stands among tall pines, feet rooted, crown lifting. Each in-breath travels up the trunk and each out-breath settles back into the ground. It closes with a single slow breath shared with the forest.

**Voice.** Warm and low, grounded but never heavy. Keep a slow, even pulse, like steady footsteps.

### The Forest Doorway

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-05.jpg`. Theme: Stepping through into the day with intention.

**Premise.** An arch of roots and ferns opens onto a sunlit path. Before stepping through, the listener picks one intention for the day and holds it in mind. The last line is a single step forward into the light.

**Voice.** Gentle, with a hint of wonder. Slow down a little at the threshold moment and leave a clear pause before the final step.

### Islands of Calm

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-06.jpg`. Theme: Finding small still places in a busy mind.

**Premise.** Rock islands rise from still, misty water. When the mind feels busy, the listener imagines stepping onto one small, quiet island per breath. By the end they notice how many calm places were there all along.

**Voice.** Soft and reassuring. Count the islands slowly and leave a full breath of silence between each.

### Pond at Dawn

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-07.jpg`. Theme: Noticing small things: ripples, reeds, one breath.

**Premise.** At the edge of a reed pond at first light, the listener notices one small thing at a time: a ripple, a reed bending, a duck gliding. Each noticing is paired with one breath. It ends by taking in the whole pond at once.

**Voice.** Quiet and curious, close to the mic without whispering. Name each detail plainly and leave space after it.

### Steady Light

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-22.jpg`. Theme: A fixed point to return to, whatever the day brings.

**Premise.** A lighthouse stands on a cliff at dawn, its light steady whatever the sea does. The listener picks a simple anchor, the breath or a word, to come back to whenever the day gets choppy. It closes by practising that return three times.

**Voice.** Calm, clear and confident. Keep the pacing steady, like the sweep of the lamp, with no rush.

### Setting Sail

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-13.jpg`. Theme: Letting the day carry you without forcing it.

**Premise.** A small sailboat sets out on a glowing lake. The listener lets the breeze, not effort, carry the boat, and practises easing any need to push the day along. It ends with the boat gliding on its own across the water.

**Voice.** Light and easy, with a small smile in the voice. Lengthen the out-breath cues.

### Warm Sands

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-14.jpg`. Theme: A body scan: warmth spreading, muscles softening.

**Premise.** A slow body scan set on sun-warmed dunes. Warmth moves from the feet up through the legs, back, shoulders and face, and each area is invited to soften. It finishes with the whole body resting in the warmth.

**Voice.** Slow and warm. Pause after naming each body area so the attention has time to arrive there.

### Morning Fog Walk

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-15.jpg`. Theme: A slow walk in the mind, one step per breath.

**Premise.** A slow walk, in the mind, through a misty pine forest at sunrise. There is one step per breath, and each step brings a new detail: cool air, soft ground, light between the trees. It closes as the fog lifts at the edge of the forest.

**Voice.** Relaxed and rhythmic. Match the phrasing to a slow walking pace and leave room for the steps.

### The Quiet Garden

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-16.jpg`. Theme: Tending your attention like a small garden.

**Premise.** The listener tends a small zen garden that stands for their attention: raking one line, placing one stone, clearing one leaf. Each small act is one breath of care. It ends by stepping back to see the tidy garden.

**Voice.** Tender and precise, with short sentences. Keep the pauses even, like the lines in raked sand.

### Carved by Water

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-17.jpg`. Theme: Patience: small daily habits shaping big change.

**Premise.** A river winds through a deep canyon it carved slowly, drop by drop. The listener reflects on one small habit they want to keep, trusting that small daily repetitions add up. It closes with the sound of the river in the imagination.

**Voice.** Patient and grounded, a little lower in pitch. Let the key line about small steps land, then leave silence.

### Wildflower Field

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-18.jpg`. Theme: Gratitude: noticing what is already blooming.

**Premise.** In a pink wildflower meadow the listener notices three things that are already going well, one per flower. Each is met with a slow breath of thanks. It ends by taking in the whole field in bloom.

**Voice.** Warm and bright but soft. Leave a generous pause after each of the three noticings.

### Let It Flow

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-23.jpg`. Theme: Release: letting what you do not need fall away.

**Premise.** A gentle waterfall pours into a still, misty pool. With each out-breath the listener lets one thing they do not need today fall with the water. It ends looking at the calm surface of the pool.

**Voice.** Soft and flowing. Stretch the out-breath cues and let the voice trail off gently at the end of phrases.

### Winter Birches

Image: `assets/track-covers/cover-24.jpg`. Theme: Stillness: quiet, bare, and clear.

**Premise.** A snowy birch grove lit by a low, soft sun. Everything is quiet and bare, and the listener lets the mind grow equally quiet and clear. It closes with a long, peaceful silence before a gentle closing line.

**Voice.** Very calm and sparing with words. This track has the most silence of any, so give the pauses their full length.

## Recording specs

- **Dry voice only.** No music, no ambience, no effects; Quiett mixes the bed in the app.
- **WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit**, mono, with peaks around -3 dBFS and no heavy compression or limiting.
- **Same mic, same room, same distance** for every track so the set sounds consistent. Record a few seconds of room tone per session.
- One file per meditation, named after the title (e.g. `above-the-mist.wav`), plus any pickups as separate files.
- **Commercial rights:** full, perpetual, worldwide rights to use, edit and distribute the recordings in Quiett and its marketing, confirmed in writing before payment.
