## 2. Voice direction

- **Calm, warm, unhurried.** Speak like a kind friend talking quietly to one person who has just woken up.
- **Natural, not performed.** Not a radio or announcer read, no "selling", and no big pitch swings.
- **Not ASMR.** No whispering, breathiness or mouth-close intimacy. Use a soft, full voice at a normal close-mic distance.
- **Keep your natural breaths.** Gentle breaths between phrases are welcome; please don't edit them out. Remove only loud clicks or lip smacks.
- **Pace: about 110 to 120 words per minute** (slow). Short sentences: let each full stop land before moving on.
- **Ellipses** ("In...") mean let the word trail and hold for a beat.
- **Ends of phrases soften downward** rather than lifting up like a question.
- **Endings:** the last line of each script should feel settled and complete, not dramatic. The app shows its own on-screen message right at 2:00, so nothing needs to be said about unlocking or finishing.
- Each meditation has a short **tone note** above its script. They are small shades of the same voice, not different characters.

## 3. Recording specs

- **Format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono.
- **Dry:** no music, no reverb, no effects, no stereo widening.
- **Consistency:** same mic, same room and same mic distance for every file, so all tracks match when they play back to back.
- **Noise floor:** room tone below about -60 dB (no hum, fans, traffic or computer noise).
- **Peaks:** below -3 dBFS, with no clipping.
- **Loudness:** mastered delivery around **-16 LUFS integrated** (gentle compression and de-essing are fine). **Please also send the raw, unprocessed take** (edited for mistakes only) of each file if you can. If you can only send one version, send the mastered one.
- **Pauses:** please **record the pauses as real silence (room tone) at the marked lengths.** Don't cut them out or shorten them. We edit each file to exactly 2:00.000 ourselves, and the timing of the pauses is part of the design.
- **Start and end:** start speaking about 0.5 s into the file. After the last line, hold the final marked pause as room tone and stop there. Don't add a long tail.
- **Length check:** each meditation should come out at about **2:00 (±5 s)** including the pauses. If a take runs much longer, speed the delivery up slightly rather than shortening the pauses.

## 6. Process

1. **Test track first:** record **First Light** only (mastered, plus raw if possible). We'll reply within about 48 hours with approval or notes.
2. **Full set after approval:** the remaining five meditations and the three Silence-mode cues, with the same setup, mic distance and levels as the approved test.
3. **One revision round included:** re-records of specific lines or files for delivery, pronunciation, timing or technical issues. Please say whether extra rounds are available.
4. **Final delivery:** all files named as above, plus a confirmation of the rights in section 5 (a line in the Fiverr delivery message is enough).

**Pronunciation notes:**
- **Quiett** is the app name. It never appears in the audio, but if it comes up, it is said like "quiet" (KWY-et).
- Every other word is plain everyday English. "Breathe" is the verb (breeth), and "Unshaken" is un-SHAY-kun.
- US or UK accent is fine. Please don't adopt a character accent.

## 7. How to read the scripts

## 8. The six scripts

### 1. First Light

- **File:** `quiett_first-light_v1.wav` · in-app id `guided:first-light`
- **Scene:** Sunrise over soft hills; an open meadow warming as the first light arrives.
- **Tone note:** Gentle and bright, like the first warm light. A soft smile in the voice.
- **Timing:** 159 words ≈ 83 s of voice at 115 wpm, plus 37 s of marked pauses, gives about **120 s (2:00)**.

Good morning. You're up, and you're here. [pause 1s]  
Let yourself be tall and easy. Let your shoulders drop. If it feels right, let your eyes close. [pause 2s]  
Breathe in slowly through your nose. [pause 3s]  
And let it go. [pause 3s]  
Picture a wide meadow, just before sunrise. The grass is cool and damp. The hills are soft and grey. [pause 2s]  
Now a thin line of gold appears along the hilltops. [pause 2s]  
With each breath in, the light grows a little. With each breath out, the meadow warms. [pause 3s]  
The light reaches the far trees. Then the grass. Then you. [pause 1s]  
Feel it on your face. There's no rush. [pause 3s]  
Notice how the light arrives. Slowly. Steadily. Without forcing anything. [pause 2s]  
You can meet your day the same way. [pause 2s]  
Choose one small thing to bring into today. Maybe patience. Maybe kindness. [pause 4s]  
Hold it gently, like warm light in your hands. [pause 2s]  
Take one last full breath in. [pause 3s]  
And let it out. [pause 2s]  
When you're ready, open your eyes. The light is already here. [pause 2s]  

*(end of track: 2:00)*

## 9. Silence-mode voice cues

These are for users who choose silence instead of a guided meditation. They are three separate short files. Don't add pauses before or after beyond about 0.3 s of room tone.

### Opening cue

- **File:** `quiett_silence-open_v1.wav`. Plays as the silent session begins. 13 words, about 7–8 s.

> Rest here in silence for the next two minutes. Upright, easy, and still.

### One-minute cue (optional)

- **File:** `quiett_silence-mid_v1.wav`. Plays at the halfway point, 1:00. 6 words, about 3–4 s.

> One minute. Stay with your breath.

### Closing cue

- **File:** `quiett_silence-close_v1.wav`. Plays a few seconds before 2:00. 11 words, about 6–7 s.

> Take one more slow breath. Your day is ready for you.
